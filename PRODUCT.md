# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students and researchers reading papers, reports, and source material on desktop or tablet. They need to move quickly between close reading, precise visual markup, searchable notes, and agent-assisted research without uploading private documents.

## Product Purpose

Mimir is a local-first PDF workspace. It makes reading and annotation feel as immediate as a native document tool while exposing the active document and its structured annotations to browser agents through WebMCP. Success means the interface disappears during reading, annotations remain portable, and agent actions stay visible and reversible.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, humane. Mimir should feel like a well-made research instrument: trustworthy at first glance, quiet during long sessions, and unusually thoughtful at the edges.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, beige “academic” theming, decorative glass panels, dark developer-console styling, floating-card overload, and novelty controls that replace familiar document affordances.

## Design Principles

1. The paper is the protagonist; chrome recedes until needed.
2. Every mutation is legible, attributable, and reversible.
3. Local-first is visible product behavior, not hidden infrastructure.
4. Human and agent workflows share one dependable command model.
5. Dense controls earn their place through speed and consistency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, complete keyboard operation, clear non-color state cues, reduced-motion support, screen-reader labels for all controls, and touch-friendly tablet annotation. Text contrast targets 7:1 where practical.
